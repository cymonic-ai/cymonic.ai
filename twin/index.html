The challenge

Most floor decisions are made on a hunch.

Layouts, rosters and opening hours shape every visit, yet they are usually changed first and measured later, if at all.

  • Problem 1

    Cameras record, nobody measures

    Footage answers “what happened?” after the fact. It rarely says where people go, wait or give up.

  • Problem 2

    Rosters lag the floor

    Staffing follows last month’s plan, so queues build at the hour nobody expected.

  • Problem 3

    Changes tested on customers

    A new layout goes live and everyone waits to see whether it helped.

How it works

From floor plan to a ranked change.

The twin is calibrated to your own days first, so its suggestions start from what really happens.

  1. 01

    Map

    Floor plan in metres: zones, tills, doors and cameras

  2. 02

    See

    People become anonymous dots on the floor

  3. 03

    Count

    Entries, dwell, occupancy and queue wait

  4. 04

    Simulate

    Calibrated to your own days, then tries changes

  5. 05

    Act

    Layouts and rosters, ranked. A person approves each one

What it measures

Flow, queues and demand, from the cameras you have.

  • Customer flow and heatmaps

    Paths, dwell and queues from existing CCTV.

  • Layout simulation

    Changes ranked by KPI before customers see them.

  • Staffing and scheduling

    A 30-minute forecast and a rule-safe roster.

  • Demand forecasting

    Visits per half-hour from counted history.

Private by design

It counts people. It never knows who they are.

  • On site

    Processed at the edge

    Frames are handled in memory on a CPU edge box in the building.

  • Nothing stored

    No images kept

    Only anonymous positions and counts leave the box.

  • Beyond retail

    One engine, four sectors

    Retail today; logistics, plants and facilities next.
