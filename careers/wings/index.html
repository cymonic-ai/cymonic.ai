Our flagship intern-to-hire programme for final-year engineering students. Real AI work from your final year, a stipend throughout, and a merit-based path to a full-time engineering role.

What you get

  • Real-world AI experience

    Hands-on AI work from the final year of your degree.

  • Senior mentors

    Work alongside senior engineers on current AI technology.

  • Stipend support

    Paid throughout the internship.

  • A path to full-time

    A direct, merit-based route to a full-time role at Cymonic.

  • Soft skills

    Communication, ownership and delivery habits.

Who can apply

Strong fundamentals, from any branch that can show them.

Open to final-year students with strong technical fundamentals — mainly from computer science and related streams, and to any student who can show real engineering skill.

Preferred streams

  • Computer Science Engineering (CSE)
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
  • Cyber Security and other CS specialisations

Other branches welcome

Any branch can apply with proof of exceptional technical and programming skill:

  • GitHub profile — active repositories and contributions
  • Coding platforms — LeetCode, HackerRank or Codeforces rankings
  • Project portfolio — deployed applications and demos
  • Certifications or hackathon wins
  • No arrears up to semester 4
  • CGPA of 70% or above
  • 2027 pass-outs

The journey

From selection to full-time engineer.

A milestone-based path from your final year to a permanent role, with dates at every step.

  1. Sep – Oct 2026

    Selection

    Clear three rounds: an online assessment, a five-hour hackathon and a final interview.

  2. Dec 2026 (tentative)

    Internship begins

    Join at the end of semester 7 for a six-month programme through your final semester.

  3. Months 3 and 6

    Performance reviews

    Two merit-based reviews of engineering skill, ownership, collaboration and fit.

  4. From June 2027

    Full-time engineer

    Selected interns join Cymonic full-time as Junior Engineers.

Where you work: month one full-time at our Kochi office; from month two, hybrid, with at least 20 hours a week in the office. Your reporting manager confirms the days.

Selection

Three rounds, each testing something different.

  1. Round 1

    Online assessment

    A proctored online test of aptitude, logical reasoning, and programming: data structures, algorithms, language basics and debugging.

    • Aptitude
    • Reasoning
    • Programming
  2. Round 2

    Five-hour hackathon

    Shortlisted students work in teams of five on a real problem, assessed live on technical skill, problem-solving, collaboration, behaviour and cultural fit.

    • Teams of 5
    • Real problem
    • Live evaluation
  3. Round 3

    Final interview

    A walk-through of your hackathon work and the reasoning behind it, then fundamentals, trade-offs, and how you learn, own work and handle setbacks.

    • Technical
    • Behavioural
    • Culture fit

Selected candidates receive an internship offer with an onboarding timeline, a mentor, and a path to full-time employment.
