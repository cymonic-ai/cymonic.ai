The challenge

Slow data work is holding AI back.

Every new request, whether a data source, a report or an AI use case, sets off the same chain of manual work. By the time it finishes, the question has often moved on.

  1. Connect the source
  2. Shape the data
  3. Build the report
  4. Test every change
  5. Document what was done

Today

Weeks of effort before anyone sees an answer.

Skilled data teams spend their time on repetitive preparation, and AI and analytics projects wait.

With Flux

Flux does that work, and shows its work.

Your people stay on the decisions that need judgment.

Why Flux changes the equation

Closing the gap between data availability and data readiness.

Flux helps organisations answer the questions that determine AI success, and keep answering them as the data estate changes. Availability is a storage problem. Readiness is a trust problem.

  • Do we know what data we have?

    Flux discovers assets and maps how they relate, so nothing hides in the estate.

  • Can we trust it?

    Quality issues, anomalies and duplication are caught before AI consumes them.

  • Who owns it?

    Ownership and stewardship are identified, so accountability has a name.

  • What information is sensitive?

    Sensitive and business-critical data is classified before it reaches a model.

  • Is it governed?

    Lineage and policy enforcement are automated rather than documented once and forgotten.

  • Is it ready for AI?

    The answer becomes a living fact about your data, not an assumption.

How Flux does it

From a plain-English request to governed, ready data.

Intelligent agents work inside your own data environment, such as your Databricks workspace or Microsoft Fabric. They plan the job, build it, follow your rules, check their own work and ask a person to approve every change.

Flux is a product, not a service.

Rather than treating data engineering as a series of one-off projects, Flux uses intelligent agents across the whole data lifecycle (Discover, Understand, Govern, Improve, Activate) to keep discovering, understanding, governing, improving and activating your data. Continuous, not one-time. The loop never stops running.

  1. Discover
  2. Understand
  3. Govern
  4. Improve
  5. Activate

What Flux takes care of

Everything your data needs to be AI-ready.

Quality, security and management are built into every asset Flux creates, not added afterwards.

  • Data quality

    • Quality checks and monitoring on every dataset
    • Duplicates and anomalies caught early

  • Sensitivity

    • Sensitive data found and labelled automatically
    • Business-critical assets classified

  • Row-level security

    • People see only the rows they are allowed to see
    • Applied consistently, not by hand

  • Column-level security

    • Protect or mask sensitive fields
    • Access follows role and sensitivity

  • Data management

    • Cataloguing, metadata and business context
    • Ownership, stewardship and lineage

  • Governance and compliance

    • Policies enforced automatically
    • Every action recorded for audit
