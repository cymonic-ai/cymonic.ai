Built to be cost-aware and safe

AI only where judgment is needed.

Every step in a flow is one of three kinds. Most are fixed and cheap; a model is used only at the real decision points.

  • Fixed step

    Skill

    A known query, rule or calculation, or an action that is already decided. No judgment needed, so no model call.

  • Judgment step

    Agent

    Real judgment over unclear input, such as assessing how serious something is and recommending what to do. Used sparingly.

  • Decision step

    Human

    A decision a person must make. Added where the work needs oversight, not for plumbing.
