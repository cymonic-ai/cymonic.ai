Built to be cost-aware and safe

Judgment only where it is needed.

Each step in a flow is one of three kinds. Most steps are fixed and cheap. A model is used only at the real decision points.

  • Fixed step

    Skill

    A known query, rule or calculation, or a tool called with a decided action. No judgment needed, so no model call.

  • Judgment step

    Agent

    Genuine judgment over ambiguous input, such as assessing severity and recommending an action. Used sparingly.

  • Decision step

    Human

    A real decision a person must make. Added where the work needs oversight, not for plumbing.
