Apex · AI-driven development lifecycle

Ship software faster,
with people in control.

Apex runs your software lifecycle as a chain of AI clones, one for each role on your team. Each takes the previous role’s work, does its part and hands on a document your team already knows, with a person reviewing every handoff.

The challenge

Faster coding is not faster delivery.

  • Problem 1

    Point tools, not lifecycle AI

    Coding assistants speed up one stage while design, testing and review stay manual. The bottleneck just moves.

  • Problem 2

    Context lost at every handoff

    The reasoning behind a decision lives in someone’s head or a chat thread, so the next role works it out again.

  • Problem 3

    All-or-nothing trust

    Tools that run the whole lifecycle offer no checkpoint. Teams trust the black box completely, or not at all.

The pipeline

A digital clone for every role.

Each clone works on its role’s inputs, in its role’s format, so the person in that role can review it the way they would a colleague’s work.

  1. 01

    Business analyst

    Writes the PRD

  2. 02

    Scrum master

    Plans the sprint

  3. 03

    Architect

    Writes the design doc

  4. 04

    Developer

    Writes code and unit tests

  5. 05

    QA / tester

    Tests and reports

  6. 06

    Reviewer

    Gives the review verdict

Not a replacement for your team, a mirror of it. Clones fill in the PRDs, plans, designs and pull requests you already use, in the tools you already use.

Human in the loop

Nothing moves on until a person says so.

Every artifact waits for the person who owns the next role. As trust grows, you can relax the gate one role at a time, without loosening it anywhere else.

  • Approve

    Move it on

    The artifact is approved and unlocks the next role.

  • Edit & approve

    Make it yours

    Change a design decision or the acceptance criteria. Your version is what moves on.

  • Reject

    Send it back

    The clone redrafts, using your comments as extra context.

Fits how you work

On top of your tools, not instead of them.

Tickets, code and pipelines stay in Jira or Azure Boards, GitHub, Azure Repos or GitLab. Documents and code are written straight into your own repositories and wikis, not copied into ours.

  • Greenfield

    Starting from a blank slate

    The architect and developer clones design and scaffold from the PRD and sprint plan.

  • Brownfield

    Working in your existing codebase

    Apex first maps your repository: structure, conventions and dependencies. Clones then extend what is there, follow your patterns and flag conflicts instead of overriding them.
