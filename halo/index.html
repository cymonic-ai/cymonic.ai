How it works

From source systems to daily action.

Your data stays where it is. HALO indexes it, understands it, and puts it to work — with no data lake to build and no migration first.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Databases, APIs and documents, each registered once and queried where it sits.

  2. 02

    Understand

    IQ enriches every table, field, document and SOP with its business meaning.

  3. 03

    Discover

    Lens builds a live KPI catalog and finds insights, cross-domain signals and opportunities.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Matrix answers questions in plain English. Pulse ranks what needs attention today.

The platform

One connected system, from raw data to daily action.

Connect your sources. IQ learns what they mean. Lens shows what they support. Matrix answers your questions, Pulse tells you first, and Crew does the routine work.

  • Connect

    Connections

    Register each source once. Adding a system means building one connector; everything above it stays the same.

  • Index

    IQ

    The semantic brain of HALO. Learns what every table, field, document and SOP means in business terms.

  • Discover

    Lens

    Generates a KPI catalog that runs live against your systems and finds insights, cross-domain signals and operational opportunities, with ideas for what to build next.

  • Ask

    Matrix

    A conversational surface over your data. Ask in natural language and get a grounded answer, within each person’s access.

  • Act

    Pulse

    A daily briefing that ranks what needs action now and what is worth knowing, so teams start with priorities.

  • Automate

    Crew

    Digital workers that follow your business process and do the routine work, with human approval where it matters.
