The platform

One connected system, from raw data to daily action.

Connect your sources. IQ learns what they mean. Lens shows what they support. Matrix answers your questions, Pulse tells you first, and Crew does the routine work.

  • Connect

    Connections

    Register each source once. Adding a system means building one connector; everything above it stays the same.

  • Index

    IQ

    The semantic brain of HALO. Learns what every table, field, document and SOP means in business terms.

  • Discover

    Lens

    Generates a KPI catalog that runs live against your systems and finds data-driven insights, cross-domain signals and operational opportunities, with ideas for what to build next.

  • Ask

    Matrix

    A conversational surface over your data. Ask in natural language and get a grounded answer, within each person’s access.

  • Act

    Pulse

    A daily briefing that ranks what needs action now and what is worth knowing, so teams start with priorities.

  • Automate

    Crew

    Digital workers that follow your business process and do the routine work, with human approval where it matters.
