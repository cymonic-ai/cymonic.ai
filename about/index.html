Before the first call

What we will and won’t do.

We will

  • Work as a senior extension of your team, with architects on the work from the first workshop to go-live.
  • Learn your data, workflows and language before we build, so what we ship fits how you already operate.
  • Design for audit, scale and handover from the first sprint, not after the demo.
  • Tell you plainly what AI will and won’t do for each use case, including the ones not worth building.

We won’t

  • Run twelve-month discovery. If we cannot get you to production in a quarter, we say so on the first call.
  • Wrap a chatbot around your problem and call it an agent.
  • Hold the system hostage. Your team owns the code, the pipelines and the runbooks at handover.
  • Take work we cannot staff with senior people. Sometimes that means we pass.
