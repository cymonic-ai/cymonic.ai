Delivery model

Every engagement follows the same path.

The order never changes; the timeline is yours. We agree it with you at the end of discovery, and each phase closes with a sign-off before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Readiness review across data, identity and process. Use cases ranked by value and risk.

    Roadmap and success measures agreed

  2. 02

    Design

    Target architecture, data and identity design, guardrails, and the sprint plan.

    Architecture signed off

  3. 03

    Build

    Sprints through our AI-driven development lifecycle (AIDLC), with a working demo at the end of each.

    Each sprint accepted on a demo

  4. 04

    Prove & launch

    Tested against the goals agreed in discovery, security-reviewed, then released.

    Go-live

  5. 05

    Run & grow

    Managed operations, observability on every agent decision, and new workflows.

    Ongoing
