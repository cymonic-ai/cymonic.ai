How we work

Most AI stalls between the demo and production. We build for the part after.

Architects lead every engagement from first workshop to go-live. You get one team that owns the strategy, the engineering and the running system — not a hand-off between three vendors.

  1. 01 — Assess

    Find where AI earns its place

    Readiness review across data, identity and process. Use cases ranked by value, risk and effort — including the ones we’d tell you not to build.

  2. 02 — Architect & build

    Engineer it like it has to last

    Agents, data pipelines and cloud-native services on Azure, with identity, guardrails and audit trails designed in from the first sprint.

  3. 03 — Operate

    Run it, measure it, improve it

    Managed operations with observability on every agent decision, delivered through our AI-driven development lifecycle (AIDLC) so each release ships faster than the last.

Delivery model

Every engagement follows the same path.

The order never changes; the timeline is yours. We agree it with you at the end of discovery, and each phase closes with a sign-off before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Readiness review across data, identity and process. Use cases ranked by value and risk.
